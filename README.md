# Meowgnificent the wizard cat — v0.11.0

Equipment is now a casual picture-first screen: Meowmalade and small equipped icons sit by their body positions on the left, with owned items in a grid on the right. Hovering or selecting an item shows only changed abilities as intuitive improvement/decline arrows, without the detailed numeric stat table or repeated long descriptions. Previewing never changes equipment, vitality, currency or skill recovery; an explicit equip action applies the chosen item. Fourteen incorrect item-to-icon atlas mappings are corrected. The animal cast is consistent across story art, portraits, field shops and the boss: Pip is a squirrel, Moss a hedgehog, Bloop an otter, the Professor an owl and the Perfect Wizard a white fox. Both shop areas gather the friends around a central picnic market. The original scenario and all fifty Korean/English narrative beats stay central and unchanged; this is a visual-cast and shop-composition update. Connected walking, game icons and shield-break presentation continue.

Korean is the default UI and story language. Settings / F10 switches Korean or English and saves the choice. The eight-area roguelite retains six fights, two shops and the final boss. Crowns and equipment belong to the current expedition; Starlight and purchased legacy upgrades survive defeat.

Download a ZIP, extract it into a new folder, and launch the executable/app. No MSI installer or CMD download command is needed.
